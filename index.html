SplitPe Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Effective Date: May 2026

This privacy policy is drafted to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and Google Play policy requirements.

1. Who We Are

SplitPe ("we", "our", or "the app") is operated by SplitPe, based in India.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the Grievance Officer:

  • Name: SplitPe
  • Email: splitpe7@gmail.com
  • Response Time: Within 30 days of receiving the request

2. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data SplitPe collects, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

3. Data We Collect

Data How Collected Purpose Required?
Email Address Provided during sign-in Authentication and account identification Yes
Mobile Phone Number Provided during setup Friend discovery and uniqueness verification Yes
UPI ID Provided during setup Generate UPI payment links Yes
Full Name Provided during setup Display name inside groups Yes
Contact Access When using "From Contacts" feature Show contacts for easier friend addition Optional
Expense & Group Data Created inside app Expense tracking and syncing Yes

4. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Your complete contact list
  • Location data
  • Photos, videos, or files
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Biometric data
  • Children's data

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide split expense functionality
  • Authenticate accounts securely
  • Generate UPI payment links
  • Sync groups and expenses
  • Respond to support requests

We do not:

  • Sell your data
  • Share data with advertisers
  • Use data to train AI models
  • Process payments directly

6. Data Sharing

Your data may be shared with:

  • Members of groups you join
  • Supabase Inc. (our backend provider)
  • Law enforcement when legally required

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

7. Data Retention

Data Retention Period
Profile & Groups Until account deletion
Session Tokens Until sign out or uninstall
Audit Logs 90 days
Deleted Account Data Deleted within 30 days

8. Your Rights Under DPDP Act

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your data
  • Withdraw consent
  • File a grievance or complaint

9. Security Measures

  • Secure token storage using Android Keystore
  • HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.2+)
  • Encrypted database storage
  • Row-Level Security protection
  • No logging of UPI payment URLs

10. Children's Privacy

SplitPe is intended only for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be communicated inside the app before they take effect.
